Regulatory Limits, Controls, and Ethics in Cyber Operations
--------------------------------------------------------------

Having studied controls such as PCI-DSS, NIST 800-53, and ISO27001, I have written reports on implementation strategies and requirements. These include requirements, fulfillment, and control mapping. This has given me insight of where these controls are applicable and why they are important in their use cases. Included are papers on regulatory requirements of PCI-DSS, risk mitigation and NIST SP 800-53, and risk mitigation specifically through (AC-3(7)).
