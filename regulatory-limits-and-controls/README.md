Governance, Risk, and Compliance in Cyber Operations
--------------------------------------------------------------

## Overview
This section highlights projects focused on cybersecurity governance, regulatory requirements, control implementation, risk reduction, and security assessment.

My work has included analysis of PCI DSS requirements, NIST SP 800-53 controls, access control implementation, control assessment methodology, and the relationship between compliance requirements and practical cybersecurity operations.

## Projects

### PCI DSS – Regulatory Requirements and Implementation Strategies
Analysis of PCI DSS requirements and how organizations implement them through governance, risk management, technical controls, scoping, gap analysis, and third-party oversight.

The report also examines how compliance requirements are translated into documented procedures and measurable security practices.

[View PCI DSS Report](./JosephPatnoe_PCI-DSS.pdf)

### NIST SP 800-53 – Risk Mitigation Through RBAC
Analysis of NIST SP 800-53 AC-3(7), Role-Based Access Control, with a focus on least privilege, separation of duties, implementation requirements, and assessment procedures under NIST SP 800-53A.

The project evaluates how an assessor would validate role definitions, permissions, IAM configurations, documentation, and access recertification.
