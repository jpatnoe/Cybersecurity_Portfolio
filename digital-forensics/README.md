Digital Forensics
-----------------------------------------------------

In lab environments, I used tools such as Autopsy, FEX Imager, HxD, and HashCalc to conduct investigations on disk images. I was able to identify evidence and suspicious artifacts. I was also able to recover deleted files, reassemble them at the byte level, and see their metadata with ExifDataView.
